Business profiles describe how Borough Furnishings evolves over time and provide the context from which problems, projects, requirements, and architectural decisions emerge.

Each profile represents a different stage in the retailer's development. The programme uses these changing business conditions to explore how engineering decisions should respond to changes in scale, capability, risk, organisation, and operational needs.

## Profile storylines

| Profile | Storyline | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Launch](./launch/)** | A successful creative side hustle becomes an organised online retailer with a dedicated shop, small team, stocked products, and in-house fulfilment. | Published; detail continues to evolve as requirements are explored. |
| **Growth** | The retailer develops a wider product range and larger or more specialised operations. | Planned |
| **Expansion** | The business operates across more markets, locations, channels, and teams. | Planned |
| **Multi-cloud evolution** | The larger retailer explores a common internal platform spanning AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. | Planned |

The profiles are not predetermined architecture stages. Each provides business context from which the relevant problems, requirements, constraints, risks, and engineering decisions will be derived.
