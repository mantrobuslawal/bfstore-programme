## About the programme

Borough Furnishings (bfstore) is a fictional retailer used to explore the end-to-end engineering of cloud-native systems across evolving business profiles.

The programme connects business context and problems to requirements, architectural decisions, implementation, validation, and evidence. Architectural choices are made in response to the needs and constraints of each business profile rather than assuming a single target architecture from the outset.

## Current status

**Programme foundations established; documentation site setup in progress.**

The initial Launch profile, its problem contexts, and five starting projects have been defined. Detailed requirements analysis, architecture, implementation, and validation will follow as the programme develops.

Business scenarios are fictional. Claims about implementation and validation will distinguish what was designed, built, tested, and demonstrated, together with relevant conditions and limitations.
